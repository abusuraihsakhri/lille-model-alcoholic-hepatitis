Lille Model

Assess response to corticosteroid treatment in severe alcohol-associated hepatitis, using the published Louvet et al. (2007) equation. All calculations run locally in this page.

Clinical inputs

Measured clearance below 40 mL/min also indicates renal insufficiency.
Enter measured PT in seconds. Choose INR above to use the published INR alternative.

Result

Enter day-0 and day-7 measurements, then calculate. A sample case is available for testing.

Clinical use: At day 7, a Lille score ≥ 0.45 suggests corticosteroid nonresponse. Decisions about continuing or discontinuing therapy require clinical assessment. Do not use this tool as an autonomous treatment order.

No patient data is sent to a server or stored in cookies or local storage. Do not enter direct identifiers.

Method: R = 3.19 − 0.101 × age + 0.147 × albumin (g/L) + 0.0165 × [day-0 − day-7 bilirubin] (µmol/L) − 0.206 × renal insufficiency − 0.0065 × baseline bilirubin (µmol/L) − 0.0096 × PT (seconds) or INR. Lille = 1 / [1 + exp(R)]. Renal insufficiency is defined as creatinine > 1.3 mg/dL or measured creatinine clearance < 40 mL/min.

Reference: Louvet A et al. Hepatology. 2007;45:1348–1354. doi:10.1002/hep.21607.
